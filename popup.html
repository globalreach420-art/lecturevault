For overnight runs: keep the laptop plugged in, set Windows sleep to Never, keep the INE tab in front and the Chrome window not minimized (Chrome pauses hidden videos). The screen may turn off. Autopilot ignores the daily data limit. It stops when you press Stop, when Stop after time is up, at Max lectures/day (if set), or when INE shows its daily viewing-limit message. Advice: no more than 30–40 videos a day, then a day off or at least a 10–14 h gap.
